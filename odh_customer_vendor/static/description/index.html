Customer Vendor Option

In earlier versions of Odoo, Contacts included separate boolean fields to easily differentiate between Customers and Vendors. However, in Odoo 13, these fields were removed, which makes it inconvenient to clearly identify and manage customers and vendors separately.

To solve this issue, we have developed this module that restores the classic customer and vendor separation functionality—just like in previous Odoo versions.

Key Features

  • Adds two separate boolean fields in Contacts:
    • Customer
    • Vendor
  • Easily identify and manage customers and vendors independently
  • Simplifies filtering, searching, and reporting
  • Improves usability for accounting, sales, and purchase workflows
  • Seamless integration with Odoo 17

Why Use This Module?

  • Ideal for users migrating from older Odoo versions
  • Eliminates confusion caused by merged partner roles
  • Enhances data clarity and operational efficiency
  • Lightweight and easy to use

Configuration

The partner model contains two boolean fields: Customer and Vendor.

The Vendor filter displays only partners marked as Vendors.

Non-vendor records are automatically converted to Vendors and available for RFQ or Purchase Order.

The Customer filter displays only partners marked as Customers.

Non-customer records are automatically converted to Customers and available for Quotations or Sale Order.
